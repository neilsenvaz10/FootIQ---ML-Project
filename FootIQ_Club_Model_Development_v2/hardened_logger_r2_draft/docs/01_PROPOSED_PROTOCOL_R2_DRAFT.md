# Prospective evaluation protocol r2 — PROPOSED DRAFT

**Status: DRAFT for owner review. Not active, not finalised, not hashed.** r1 (`matched_v1_prospective_r1/09_PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md`, hash in `PREREGISTRATION_r1.json`) remains the only registered protocol until the owner approves this text; if approved, this file is frozen, hashed, and recorded in a new pre-registration **before the first official batch**. It never applies retroactively and never to shadow runs. Clause numbers R2.n are referenced by the AA-1…AA-14 matrix.

# PART A — Scientific evaluation (unchanged from r1)

**A.1 Models (fixed).** `v2_frozen` (sha256 `55c1e4c5…3e56c`), `v1_matched` (`33bd80f6…ec34`), `v1_original` (`067426e8…c5b9`), `v1_elo` (descriptive).
**A.2 Comparisons.** Primary contrast `v2_frozen` − `v1_matched`; secondary: `v2_frozen` − `v1_original`, `v1_matched` − `v1_original`. Comparators are not changed after the hash.
**A.3 Metrics.** Primary: multiclass log loss (paired difference per contrast). Secondary: multiclass Brier, accuracy, macro-F1 (argmax and the declared draw-weight 1.45 rule), calibration (10-bin reliability per class, ECE, intercept/slope for home-win and draw), league groups (tier 1/2/3+; regions; split-year vs calendar-year; groups under 300 matches listed but not interpreted), missing-history strata (min prior matches < 10, 10–29, ≥ 30; first season in competition; coverage flags), draw probability quality (mean predicted vs actual draw rate, AUC, binary draw log loss, deciles, Elo-gap bands).
**A.4 Uncertainty.** Paired cluster bootstrap over competition × calendar month, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261021, percentile 95% intervals; no multiplicity adjustment for secondary items; claims limited to the primary contrast.
**A.5 Gates (unchanged).** The first analysis checkpoint requires **≥ 5,000 primary-eligible fixtures with confirmed outcomes, from ≥ 25 competitions, spanning ≥ 3 calendar months**. Second checkpoint at ≥ 10,000; hard end at 12 months from the start of Stage 2 (R2.1) or 15,000 eligible results, whichever is first; no early stopping for efficacy; harm monitor after ≥ 2,000 eligible results (primary contrast > +0.010 with the 95% interval above zero → owner informed; models still not modified); descriptive success criteria: primary interval entirely below zero and point estimate ≤ −0.002, tier-1/tier-2 groups with > 2,000 matches not worse than `v1_matched` by > 0.005, ECE not worse by > 0.005, secondary contrast 1 not above zero; inconclusive is reported as inconclusive.
**A.6 Eligible population (conditions of r1, as modified by Part B).** (a) both clubs ≥ 5 prior rated matches and Elo-connected; (b) no identity-collision club (Police FC, Vision FC); (c) no unresolved past-dated fixture for either club; (d) competition's latest completed result ≤ 14 days before the fixture; (e) regular-round domestic league; (f) sealed before the deadline in R2.10 (**replaces r1 (f)**); (g) confirmed 90-minute label (R2.7). Everything else is logged with its reasons and reported as a stratum, never pooled silently.

# PART B — Operational rules (new in r2)

**R2.1 Stages and scope (AA-1).** *Stage 1 — Bundesliga operational pilot*: official batches are limited to `deutschland:1-bundesliga`, season 2026-27, data source OpenLigaDB `bl1`/2026 (S2) with OpenFootball (S1) as reference. Stage 1 results **count toward the A.5 sample**, but no inferential model-comparison conclusion is produced in Stage 1 (see the separate pilot protocol). *Stage 2 — scientific evaluation* begins with the first official batch under a later amendment that admits further competitions; the 12-month and 15,000-result limits run from Stage 2 start; analysis checkpoints follow A.5 with the unchanged 25-competition gate. Competition scope is closed: a competition/season/source combination not named in this clause or a later approved amendment is rejected by the logger.
**R2.2 Accepted sources (competition-specific, AA-1/AA-5).** S1 OpenFootball (CC0, pinned commits) — all competitions in the frozen universe; S2 OpenLigaDB (ODbL 1.0) — `bl1`/2026 only; S3 Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) — independent confirmation only, never sole source, never in features, never a source of dates or times, unused until the owner approves its licensing document. A snapshot is accepted only if every record has league `bl1` and season 2026; any other content is rejected as a whole.
**R2.3 Newer OpenFootball snapshots (AA-2).** A new S1 snapshot enters only through the ingestion run, producing a hashed merged-universe frame (record id, date, entities, score, retrieval time, snapshot sha256). De-duplication is by (competition, season, home, away): S1 and S2 agreeing on score and date (≤ 1 day) → a single canonical record with `agreement = two_sources_agree`; disagreement is a conflict (R2.5). Until such a frame exists the pinned S1 universe is the only S1 input.
**R2.4 Canonical record and ordering date (AA-3).** When S1 and S2 agree, both records are kept; the canonical score and ordering date are S1's; the canonical kickoff time and result type are S2's. S2-only matches use the date of the S2 UTC kickoff. A date difference of one day between sources is logged; more than one day is a conflict.
**R2.5 Conflicts, withdrawals and corrections (AA-4).** A conflict is: score disagreement between sources or between snapshots; a completed result later reported as not completed (withdrawal); a score changed in a later snapshot (correction); a date disagreement > 1 day; a duplicate ordered pair. Nothing is resolved automatically (no majority vote, no source ranking): the pair is quarantined, both clubs are flagged `incomplete_or_inconsistent_history` and their fixtures are not primary-eligible until a human adjudication is recorded in an append-only log; adjudication never alters a sealed prediction. A newer snapshot may never silently remove a previously verified result.
**R2.6 Incomplete history from rejected played matches (AA-4).** Any played match that cannot enter the feature state — unresolved or ambiguous identity, no explicit 90-minute result type, kickoff not before retrieval, a match known to the frozen universe but absent from the source, a fixture removed from the newest snapshot — is an *incomplete-history event*: the clubs concerned (the whole competition if they cannot be identified) are not primary-eligible until it is resolved.
**R2.7 Regulation-time labels and confirmation (AA-6).** A confirmed 90-minute label requires: explicit regulation-time type (S2 `After90Minutes`; S1 per the v1.1 label rules); status completed (not awarded, abandoned, postponed, cancelled or unclassified); the same score from two admitted sources (at least one of S1/S2), or from one S1/S2 source in two snapshots at least 24 h apart with the first retrieved at least 48 h after kickoff; no disagreement at any time. Scores typed Unknown, after stoppage time, extra time or penalties are never labels. Operators poll at least 48 h and again at least 72 h after the last kickoff of each matchday. A fixture is evaluated only if confirmed by the analysis checkpoint; unconfirmed fixtures are counted by reason.
**R2.8 Retrieval-time and feature rules (AA-4/AA-11).** Every snapshot carries a retrieval timestamp and sha256 verified before each use. Snapshots must be strictly ordered by retrieval time with monotone content; an out-of-order snapshot is rejected. A result enters a prediction's features only if its snapshot was retrieved at or before the prediction timestamp and its kickoff precedes that retrieval. Features come only from S1/S2, for **all models, one merged history and one cutoff**; the merged-state hash is stored in each record. The 14-day competition freshness rule is unchanged.
**R2.9 Kickoff snapshot freshness (AA-7).** The UTC kickoff used for sealing comes from an S2 snapshot retrieved no more than 24 h before the prediction timestamp; the same snapshot supplies the results used. If two S2 snapshots give kickoffs differing by more than 30 minutes the earlier applies. A fixture without a source-stated UTC kickoff is not sealed.
**R2.10 Prediction timestamps and sealing (AA-8/AA-9/AA-10).** The prediction timestamp is the system clock at execution time (UTC); callers cannot supply it; it must not precede the last sealed prediction, the newest snapshot's retrieval, or the approval time, and the newest poll must show a clock offset ≤ 120 s against the server's HTTP Date. A prediction is sealed at least 60 minutes before the stated kickoff; fixtures past their deadline or already completed are rejected and never logged (no backdating). This clause supersedes r1 condition (f) for fixtures with a source-stated UTC kickoff. **Per fixture and model version only the first sealed prediction is evaluated**; duplicates are refused by the ledger.
**R2.11 Rescheduled, postponed, cancelled and identity-changed fixtures (AA-11).** Sealed records are never edited or deleted; a hash-chained side-car records: moved later (flag `rescheduled_later`; 7 days or more `postponed_7_days_or_more`; the 14-day freshness rule is re-checked against the new date and a failing fixture becomes ineligible); moved earlier so that the seal is later than the new kickoff − 60 min (ineligible); identity changed in two consecutive snapshots (ineligible); absent from two consecutive newest snapshots (ineligible, treated as cancelled/removed); result conflict (ineligible until adjudicated). Postponed/cancelled states are inferred because OpenLigaDB has no explicit flag.
**R2.12 Late-arriving results (AA-12).** A match already played at sealing time involving either club and first reported afterwards is flagged `late_arriving_history_result`; the prediction stays eligible and a sensitivity analysis excluding such predictions is reported.
**R2.13 Missing results (AA-13).** A fixture without a confirmed result is `pending` (not excluded) from kickoff; it becomes ineligible only at an analysis checkpoint, counted by reason; no rolling exclusion.
**R2.14 Provenance, integrity and licensing (AA-14).** Every record stores: input snapshots (id, sha256, retrieval time), merged-state sha256, the sha256 of the artifact manifest (model artifacts, preprocessing, Elo parameters, feature contracts, feature-generation code, adapters, logger code, frozen universe, exclusions), model hashes, kickoff source, attribution and licence, and its own sha256. Attribution: S2 "Daten von OpenLigaDB (www.openligadb.de), lizenziert unter ODbL 1.0"; S1 repository/commit/file-line; S3 page, revision, permalink and CC BY-SA notice. Contact information is never written to records, snapshots, ledgers, reports or tracked files. Retrieval follows each source's current terms.
**R2.15 Ledger and approval.** Official predictions live in an append-only, hash-chained, atomically written ledger with an external anchor; interrupted runs are detected and recovered explicitly; deletion of any batch (including the newest) is detected. Official mode is off by default and runs only with the owner's approval file, which must name protocol r2 and its sha256, the exact model hashes, the artifact-manifest sha256, competition and season, an expiry, a sealing window and a maximum number of fixtures (optionally matchdays). Empty, malformed, expired or mismatched approvals are rejected.
