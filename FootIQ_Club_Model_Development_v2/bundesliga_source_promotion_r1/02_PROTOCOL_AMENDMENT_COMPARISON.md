## Changes that alter results of the evaluation (not just paperwork)
Rows 4, 5 and 6: which results enter features, when predictions may be sealed, and what counts as a label. Rows 2 and 8 shrink or widen the eligible population by source. Everything else is documentation.

## Recommended final amendment (not activated) — differences from draft 05b
1. **A1.1** S2 scope reads "OpenLigaDB for league-seasons on the validated list; at activation: `bl1`/2026 only. Adding a league requires a validation run (≥ 99.5% exact agreement on overlap, 0 unexplained conflicts, 0 identity ambiguities) and owner approval." S3 stays confirmation-only, no live use until the licensing document is approved.
2. **New A1.3b (agreement canonicalisation):** when S1 and S2 agree, both records are kept; the canonical **score and date** come from S1 if present, otherwise S2; the canonical **kickoff time and result type** come from S2. A canonical record is never created from a pair that is in conflict.
3. **A1.5.1 (as-of rule, explicit):** a result is usable only if its `retrieved_utc` ≤ the prediction timestamp and its kickoff precedes its `retrieved_utc`; the merged snapshot id and sha256 are logged.
4. **A1.6c:** the UTC kickoff used for the sealing deadline must come from an S2 snapshot retrieved ≤ 24 h before sealing.
5. **A1.6d:** if a later snapshot moves the kickoff to a time earlier than the sealing time, the prediction is flagged `kickoff_moved_before_seal` and excluded from the primary population (it is not deleted).
6. **A1.2** adds `adapter_version`, request-ledger identifier and `plan_sha256` of the retrieval to the provenance fields.
7. **New A1.9 (source-terms compliance):** retrieval must follow each source's current terms (OpenLigaDB: change-date polling, ≤ 60 requests/min, jitter, back-off, descriptive User-Agent with contact; Wikimedia policies); polling stays manual unless the owner approves a schedule; attribution strings accompany any published table.
8. **A1.8:** unchanged (inactive until approved; new protocol hash; before the first official batch only).
