## 3. Serie A: relegation positions, play-off procedures and the one gap
* **Relegation: verified.** NOIF art. 49(1)(a) (FIGC text updated to Comunicato Ufficiale 209/A of 6 May 2026, sha256 `a3e228d4e07a4f6e1b09cd716d03aa5265d22e48f242ac3c4274875b792a0c6d`): "Girone unico di 20 squadre"; the teams classified 18th, 19th and 20th are relegated to Serie B.
* **The 2026/27 decision, read in full on 2026-10-26** (FIGC Comunicato Ufficiale n. 244/A, Federal Council of 26 May 2026, published 27 May 2026; 94,317 bytes, sha256 `cace74eac2fe039b9f61f37556fe0e429caddc7046cdd386166f36254bcfc6bf`; derogation from NOIF art. 51 for the 2026/27 season):
  1. (i) **Two clubs level on points for the title:** one match at the home ground of the club better placed in the *classifica avulsa*; no extra time; penalties if level.
  2. (ii) **17th and 18th level on points:** two legs; the better avulsa club hosts the return; more goals over the two legs wins; otherwise penalties (no extra time).
  3. (iii) **More than two clubs level on points**, both to find the two clubs for those play-offs and "to determine all the other positions": the avulsa: (a) points in the matches between all clubs of the avulsa, (b) goal difference in those matches, (c) overall goal difference, (d) overall goals scored, (e) a draw by lots.
  4. (iv) If a club in these play-offs is a **UEFA competition finalist**, the avulsa of (iii) decides directly, with no play-off.
* **The gap (needs a source or your decision):** (i) to (iv) say nothing about **exactly two clubs level on points in any other place** (for example 4th or 12th): clause (iii) is written for "more than two clubs". NOIF art. 51 (base text, no. 7) for Serie A used to require play-offs for UEFA places; CU 244/A replaces that with the avulsa only where it says so. An avulsa for two clubs would reduce to head-to-head points, head-to-head goal difference, overall goal difference, goals scored, lots, but that is an inference by analogy, **not** a verified rule. Until the Lega's competition regulation or a FIGC text says so, such ties stay **unresolved** in any future Serie A profile.
* **What blocks the profile now:** (a) engineering (section 7); (b) data: Serie A is Not eligible (20 played-date fixtures without a result, stale); (c) scope: the points-only mode and its ranges are approved for the Bundesliga and La Liga only; (d) the gap above; (e) points penalties and sports-justice results are not in our data.
* **Sports-justice rule for a match that did not finish regularly:** Codice di giustizia sportiva (FIGC, 2025 edition, file `figc_cgs_2025.pdf`), **art. 10**. (1) A club held responsible for facts that affected or prevented the regular running of a match **loses it 0-3**, or keeps the score the opponent had on the pitch if that is better for the opponent. (5) For facts that cannot be judged on technical criteria alone, the sports-justice bodies may: (a) declare the match regular with the pitch result, (b) impose the loss of the match, (c) order the match to be repeated, or (d) in exceptional circumstances annul it and order it to be played. (4) A club that fails to turn up on time loses 0-3 and also loses one point. (2), (9) Point penalties of this kind also exist for specified offences. **Consequence for the simulator:** the result of any abandoned, suspended or disputed Serie A match is a decision of a sports-justice body, so it can never be inferred from the fixture list, and point penalties are not in our data either.

## 4. Ligue 1: the 2026/27 text was found and read (2026-10-26)
* **How it was found:** a Wikipedia page on the 2026-27 Ligue 1 season cites an lfp.fr document; Wikipedia was used only as a pointer. I downloaded the document from lfp.fr and read the articles quoted below in the PDF text (the page's own tie-break list was not used as the rule).
* **Source (primary):** LFP, *Statuts et Règlements* 2026/27 (file name dated 08/09/2026; HTTP `Last-Modified` 2026-09-08 07:52 UTC), `https://www.lfp.fr/assets/26_27_LFP_STATUTS_REGLEMENTS_08_09_2026_f2aaaaa325.pdf`, 2,611,062 bytes, **sha256 `c57b88f6fb997d4cc5d2f116eaf3afdc0a14670787d38875984329303fc04871`** (saved under `data/supervised/rules_research/`, git-ignored).
* **Articles read:**
  * **Art. 518:** 18 clubs, one group, 17 matches in each half (34 matchdays).
  * **Art. 518 bis:** 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a defeat; a forfeit counts as 3-0; a match lost by penalty (art. 560) removes the points and cancels the goals scored by that team; a match that was stopped and must be replayed in full awards no point and no goal until it is replayed.
  * **Art. 518 ter (tie-break), the same list for two or more clubs level on points:** (1) overall goal difference; (2) points in the matches between them; (3) goal difference in the matches between them; (4) goals scored overall; (5) most wins; (6) most away wins; (7) fewest disciplinary points (yellow card 1, red card 3); (8) a draw by lots. Criteria 2 and 3 apply only if **both** matches between the clubs have been played; until then criteria 4 to 7 apply in priority. (At the end of a complete season both matches have always been played, so for final positions the order is 1 to 8.)
  * **Art. 519:** at the end of the last matchday the **last two clubs (17th and 18th) are relegated**; the **16th** plays a two-leg barrage against the winner of the Ligue 2 play-offs (3rd, 4th and 5th of Ligue 2), the winner playing in Ligue 1 the next season. **Art. 519 ter:** the barrage's first leg is at the lower division's ground, the second at the higher division's; more goals over two legs wins; if level, extra time of 2 x 15 minutes in the second leg, then penalties.
  * **Art. 520:** reinstatement (repêchage) rules; **art. 521:** a club excluded or declared in general forfeit during the season is classified last and **the goals and points of the other clubs from their matches against it are annulled**; **art. 518 quater:** early stop of the competitions (four cases, performance index).
  * **Art. 548, 549, 549 bis:** a match not started or stopped for weather resumes the next day; after a definitively interrupted match for serious incidents the LFP Discipline Commission decides: replay, resumption from the minute of interruption, uphold the score at that moment, or loss by penalty.
* **Comparison with the 2025/26 rulebook** that was read earlier (sha256 `eaa8f371…1b591`): the text of art. 518 bis and 518 ter is **identical** after normalising the file encoding (2,552 characters each). Art. 549 bis is unchanged in substance.
* **Result:** the 2026/27 Ligue 1 rules are **verified from the primary text**. The profile **stays blocked** (not activated, per your instruction): engine, data and scope reasons in section 7. `config/season_rules.json` now says so instead of "text not found".

## 5. Cancelled, suspended and abandoned matches (decision D7)
What each rulebook says (the outcome is never inferable from the match list):

| League | Rule found | Who decides the result |
|---|---|---|
| Bundesliga | SpOL para 14, no. 4-5: a match abandoned without fault is **replayed at the same venue**; if a team is at fault it is awarded **0:2 / 2:0**, or the better score the innocent side had at the time stands; a match won on the field but later awarded is entered as 2:0 | DFB legal bodies (no. 6) |
| La Liga | Bases: a suspended match is replayed within 24 hours if the cause ends, otherwise LALIGA sets a new date after hearing the clubs and the RFEF | RFEF disciplinary bodies for abandonment (code not read) |
| Premier League | L.15: abandoned after kick-off but before 90 minutes: the Board either **counts the score at abandonment** (points per C.2) or **orders a replay**; before kick-off: re-arranged; fault is a breach of the rules (L.17); curtailment of a competition: C.25-C.30 | The Premier League Board |
| Serie A | NOIF: a suspended match is played as the competent League decides; CGS art. 10: loss 0-3 (or the opponent's better pitch score), repetition, annulment or regular result | Sports-justice bodies (CGS art. 10, read) |
| Ligue 1 | **2026/27** art. 549 bis: after a definitively interrupted match the LFP Discipline Commission may order a **replay**, a **resumption**, **uphold the score** or award a **loss by penalty**; art. 518 bis no. 3 and 4 (penalty loss removes points and goals; a match to be replayed in full awards nothing until replayed); art. 521 (exclusion: results against that club annulled) | LFP Discipline Commission and Competitions Commission |

**Consequence (unchanged and approved):** a league with any cancelled or abandoned fixture stays **ineligible** until the official decision exists in our data. To lift that safely the store needs a recorded outcome per such fixture (replay date, upheld score, awarded score, or curtailment) with the source reference and date, and a points-adjustment record for penalties; then the simulator treats a replay as a remaining fixture, an upheld or awarded score as a completed result (with the stated goals), and anything undecided as blocking. Today no such fixture exists in the five leagues.

## 7. What engine support would need (requirements only; nothing implemented, nothing activated)
**Ligue 1** (rules verified):
1. New criteria in the engine: most wins and most away wins (both are exact in a points-only simulation, because they depend only on win / draw / loss), fair-play points (card data that the store does not hold: unresolved) and a final draw by lots (never simulated: unresolved).
2. Criteria list `goal_difference, h2h_points, h2h_goal_difference, goals_scored, wins, away_wins, fair_play, lots` for two or more clubs; head-to-head over the clubs' two matches. For three or more clubs the text does not say how "the matches between them" are formed, so the position ranges would use only overall goal difference (`bounds_multi_club_prefix` 1), as for the Bundesliga.
3. Bands: title 1, top four 1 to 4 (positions only), **direct relegation 17 and 18**; the 16th-place barrage is not simulated and not part of "relegation".
4. A record of administrative results: matches lost by penalty (points and goals removed), forfeits (3-0), a club excluded or in general forfeit (results against it annulled), replays; otherwise the league stays Not eligible.
5. Fresh results (the current data are stale, and live access needs your separate approval) and a scope decision.
**Serie A** (rules verified, one gap):
1. A **position-dependent decider**: exactly two clubs level for 1st place, or for 17th and 18th, are decided by a play-off, never by goal difference, so the engine must mark them as an unresolved decider before applying any criterion; the avulsa then only orders the host.
2. The avulsa for three or more clubs over the original group (points in the matches between them, goal difference in those matches, overall goal difference, overall goals scored, lots) and the choice of the two play-off clubs.
3. Data on UEFA finalists (clause iv); a points-adjustment record; the gap about exactly two clubs in other places.
4. Fresh results and a scope decision.
