FootIQ college mini-project

Football analytics and match-outcome prediction for six competitions: the five domestic leagues and the UEFA Champions League. Predictions come from existing frozen models; each carries a validation badge.

Data attribution: club results from openfootball (CC0-1.0) and, for the Champions League, partly from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0); Bundesliga and La Liga results are refreshed from OpenLigaDB (ODbL-1.0) when FootIQ starts. Historical evaluation numbers quoted in the report were inspected during development and are not new independent evidence.
